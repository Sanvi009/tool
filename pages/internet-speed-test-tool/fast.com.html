About fast.com

fast.com is a free internet speed test service created by Netflix. It's designed to be simple, fast, and ad-free. The tool measures your current download speed from Netflix's servers.

Key Features

  • Simple Interface: One-click speed test with minimal distractions
  • No Registration: Use immediately without signing up
  • Ad-Free: Clean experience with no advertisements
  • Mobile Friendly: Works perfectly on smartphones and tablets
  • Global Servers: Tests from Netflix servers worldwide

How to Use

  1. Click the "Open fast.com Now" button above
  2. The test starts automatically when the page loads
  3. Wait a few seconds for your download speed result
  4. Click "Show more info" for upload speed and latency

Why Use fast.com?

fast.com is ideal for quick speed checks, especially if you use Netflix. Since it tests from Netflix's servers, it gives you a good indication of your streaming performance.
